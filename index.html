About Our Society

Rooted in Unity, Caring for Community

Our Mission

To bring together Filipinos and friends of all nationalities in Sioux Lookout, promoting cultural preservation, mutual support, and community development.

Bayanihan Spirit

We embody the cherished tradition of Bayanihan—communal unity and cooperation—helping newcomers settle comfortably in Northern Ontario.

Cultural Heritage

Celebrating Philippine heritage while welcoming residents of all nationalities to contribute to the wonderful multicultural tapestry of Sioux Lookout.
